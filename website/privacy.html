Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2025

BhashaSarthi does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your translations stay on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BhashaSarthi does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Any personally identifiable information

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only, using AsyncStorage. We have no access to it:

  • Translation history — your last 50 translations, stored on-device and never transmitted to our servers
  • Last translation — the most recent translation is cached to restore your session

You can delete all stored data at any time via Settings → Clear History.

3. Data Transmitted to Third Parties

When you perform a translation or use voice input, the text or audio is sent to Google Gemini API to perform the translation. This is the only external service used.

  • Text you submit for translation is processed by Google Gemini AI
  • Voice recordings are sent to Google Gemini API for transcription and translation
  • This data transmission is governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • We do not store, log, or have access to what you send to or receive from Google Gemini

4. Microphone Access

BhashaSarthi requests microphone permission only when you use the voice input feature. The microphone is not accessed at any other time. Audio recordings are sent directly to Google Gemini API for transcription and are not stored locally or on our servers.

5. No Analytics or Tracking

BhashaSarthi does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. There are no third-party analytics libraries in the app.

6. Children's Privacy

BhashaSarthi is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

7. Open Source

BhashaSarthi is fully open source under the MIT License. You can review the complete source code at github.com/theshivam7/BhashaSarthi.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
